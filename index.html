Juris Games

Free, open-source apps with no ads, no accounts and no tracking.

Tasks: shared to-do and shopping lists that sync device to device. Android and Windows.

Sobres: a shared envelope budget for two or more people, device to device. Android and Windows.

Ronda: live quizzes and polls everyone answers on their phone.

Sumate: sign-up sheets: who brings what, shifts, volunteers.

Juntos: video calls in the browser, no time limit.

QR: scan and make QR codes, with a history that stays on your phone. Android, and making codes in the browser.
